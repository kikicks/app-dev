# Iron Man (2008)

> "I am Iron Man." — *Tony Stark*

## Movie Overview

**Iron Man** is a 2008 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. Produced by Marvel Studios and distributed by Paramount Pictures, it is the **first film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU)**.

## Plot Summary

1. **The Capture:** Industrialist and genius inventor Tony Stark is captured by a terrorist group called the *Ten Rings* in Afghanistan.
2. **Mark I Creation:** With the help of fellow captive Yinsen, Tony builds a powered suit of armor to escape captivity.
3. **Refining the Suit:** Upon returning home, he perfects his armor design to create the iconic red-and-gold **Mark III** suit.
4. **The Final Conflict:** Tony uncovers a betrayal by his business partner, Obadiah Stane, leading to a high-stakes battle between Iron Man and Iron Monger.

---

## Notable Armor Versions Featured

- **Mark I:** Built in a cave from scrap materials.
- **Mark II:** Prototype testing aerodynamic silver design.
- **Mark III:** Final iconic gold and hot-rod red armor.
